The heat is on: how crop growth, development, and yield respond to high temperature

Zhu T*, Fonseca de Lima CF*, De Smet I.

Curated and integrated historical temperature records from NASA GISS for 5 cities across distinct climate zones (Sydney, Shanghai, Brussels, New York, Rio de Janeiro). When drought meets heat — a plant omics perspective

Xu X, Fonseca de Lima CF, Vu LD, De Smet I.

Independently designed and produced Fig. 1, integrating three environmental time-series — air temperature anomaly, soil humidity at 50 cm depth (%), and precipitation increments (mm) — from the USDA SCAN network (Shagbark Hills station, Iowa).
